## About This Series

This series aims for the level of understanding held by "the top 1% of infrastructure engineers, even among those working at the leading infrastructure companies like AWS and Google." Rather than just memorizing operational steps, the goal is to thoroughly explain:

- The internal mechanics and design rationale behind why something is built the way it is
- How to actually diagnose and troubleshoot the problems that come up in production

...in a way that lets even a beginner climb the ladder one step at a time. When a topic gets too large, we don't force it into a single article — we split it by theme and link the articles together.

Every article is written to stand on its own. A deep-dive article never links a reader back to the article it grew out of — reading in whatever order interests you is the priority. A foundational article, on the other hand, links forward to any deep-dive articles that grew out of it, so follow those links whenever you want to go further on a topic.

As the article count has grown, this page no longer tries to manage a reading order across every single article. Instead, it points you toward three entry points, chosen by what you're actually trying to do.

## The Site's Three Entry Points

### 📚 Top 1% (Homepage)

The [homepage](/en) lists every article, grouped by series. You can also filter by series, or by content type (main articles, hands-on labs, audio lectures, audio reviews). **Use this when you want to see everything a series has to offer, or when you just want to browse whatever topic catches your eye.**

### 🎓 University

[The University](/en/university) reorganizes the articles around the units of faculty, department, and grade — a roadmap meant to be followed in order. It maps out the distance from complete beginner to an AWS/Google-caliber top engineer, as a sequence of grade levels. **Use this when you're not sure where to start, or when you want to learn systematically.** At the moment, seven departments have a fully complete grade ladder ("fully open"): Active Directory, AWS, Ansible/IaC, VPN, DNS, Mail, and Windows Server. Other departments will fill in their grades gradually as more content gets added.

### 🔍 Search

[Search](/en/search) lets you search by keyword across every article. **Use this when you already know exactly what you're looking for — it's the fastest path there.**

## Not Sure Where to Start?

- Want to learn systematically, in order → [The University](/en/university)
- Want to freely browse a specific series or topic → [The Homepage](/en)
- Already know what you're looking for → [Search](/en/search)

## Series List

The full list of articles per series, with filtering, is available on [the homepage](/en). Every article opens by noting which number it is within its series, along with a link to its prerequisite article, so you never lose your way no matter which article you start from.
